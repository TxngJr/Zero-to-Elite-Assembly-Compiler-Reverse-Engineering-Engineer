# Exercises

## A. Decimal/Binary — 20
แปลง: 0,1,2,3,5,7,8,10,13,15,16,31,32,42,63,64,65,100,127,255

## B. Binary/Decimal — 20
`0,1,10,11,101,111,1000,1010,1111,10000,10001,101010,111111,1000000,1000001,01111111,10000000,10100111,11111110,11111111`

## C. Hex — 20
`0x0,0x1,0x7,0xA,0xF,0x10,0x1F,0x20,0x2A,0x3F,0x40,0x41,0x7F,0x80,0xA7,0xFE,0xFF,0x100,0x1234,0xDEADBEEF`

## D. Two's complement — 15
ระบุ unsigned/signed ของ `00000000,00000001,00101010,01111110,01111111,10000000,10000001,10100000,11000000,11110000,11111011,11111100,11111101,11111110,11111111`.

## E. Bitwise — 20
คำนวณ AND/OR/XOR/NOT, shifts, masks bits 0/1/3/7, set/clear/toggle/test bit 5, extract nibbles, combine nibbles และอธิบายข้อจำกัด signed shifts.

## F. Endianness/Memory — 10
วาง bytes ของ `0x1234`, `0x12345678`, reconstruct จาก `78 56 34 12`, ตรวจ alignment 4/8-byte, virtual vs physical, address range ของ object.

## G. CPU model — 10
อธิบาย register, ALU, instruction pointer, instruction vs source statement, ISA vs microarchitecture, Fetch/Decode/Execute, modern CPU caveats และ compiler→ISA.
